## Items

1. **Ending a nap leaves the nap on screen.** Seen 30 Oct: a nap was set (everything shown was
   right), then cancelled. The alarms were cancelled, but the main screen still showed the nap
   and the bottom bar still offered End nap, until the app was force-closed and reopened.
   Ending a nap (End nap, its last alarm stopped) should clear the Napping card and the bar's nap
   state straight away.
   - Cause found (not built yet): the main screen only redraws on state it tracks, and the nap's
     alarms aren't part of it. A fix is set aside (git stash, "v0.4.6 item 1 fix") until the list
     is final.

2. **"I'm awake" on mornings with no alarms, by hand and automatically.** Seen 30 Oct (Friday):
   no alarms, Sleep In on. Getting up, there was no good way to wake the house and the phone;
   Good Morning - App had to be run by hand. It ended Sleep Focus, made the announcement and
   turned Awake For Day on, as it should, but **Sleep In stayed on** (problem).
   - Cause found (read-only): on alarm mornings Sleep In is handled by the stop flow; on a
     no-alarm morning nothing turns it off. Good Morning - App doesn't touch it, and the
     integration only turns it off on undo bedtime.
   - **By hand:** "I'm awake" in the app on mornings with no alarms (bottom bar, ⋯, a Siri phrase,
     a Lock Screen / Action button control). It tells Home Assistant, which turns Sleep In off,
     Awake For Day on, disarms, and sends RUN_GOOD_MORNING so Good Morning - App runs.
   - **Automatically:** Michael's idea: out of bed → bedroom door opens → down the hall (hallway
     presence) → push to run Good Morning. The risk: a glass of water, letting the dog out, or
     Seth still in bed (he wouldn't want the bedroom curtains opening). **Decided:** see the plan below.
   - Entities read back 30 Oct: bed `binary_sensor.master_bedroom_bed_presence_bed_occupied_left`
     (Michael) and `…_right` (Seth); bedroom door `binary_sensor.aqara_door_and_window_sensor_door_2`;
     hallway `binary_sensor.hallway_master_presence`, `binary_sensor.aqara_presence_multisensor_fp300_occupancy`,
     `binary_sensor.upstairs_occupancy`.

   **Plan (decided 30 Oct).** Only on mornings with no alarms, while home (alarm mornings
   already wake the house when the alarms are stopped):

   1. **Leaving the bedroom, any hour → disarm only.** Out of bed (left side) → bedroom door
      opens → hallway presence: Home Assistant sends `RUN_DISARM` at once. Nothing else changes
      (no lights, curtains or Focus). This is what makes letting the dog out through the patio
      door (`binary_sensor.aqara_door_and_window_sensor_door_4`) safe: the disarm starts while
      you're still in the hallway.
   2. **Back in bed within a few minutes → re-arm.** `RUN_ARM_STAY` once back in bed for the
      existing *Re-arm after back in bed for* time, as after a temporary alarm. A water or dog
      trip ends here.
   3. **From 8:00 AM, once it's clearly real → good morning.** When you've been up 15 min without
      getting back in bed, or the phone comes off the charger (companion battery state Charging →
      Not Charging), Home Assistant sends an actionable push **"Up for the day?"** with
      *Good morning* / *Back to bed*. No answer and still up 10 min later → it goes ahead.
      Good morning = Sleep In off, Awake For Day on, `RUN_GOOD_MORNING` (Good Morning - App:
      Sleep Focus off, the announcement). *Back to bed* = nothing more (re-arm as in 2 once in
      bed).
   4. **"I'm awake" by hand, any time:** in the app (bottom bar and ⋯ on a no-alarm morning), a
      Siri phrase, and a Lock Screen / Action button control. Runs good morning (as in 3) at once.

   Settings (app, with defaults shown): earliest time 8:00 AM; up for 15 min; ask-first wait
   10 min; the automatic part on/off.

   **Later (not this version):** a quiet good morning when Seth is still in bed (right side
   occupied): everything except the bedroom curtains and the Master Bedroom announcement, likely a
   separate `RUN_GOOD_MORNING_QUIET` for the shortcut. Seth isn't home right now.

3. **The main screen changes on time, not only on the next redraw.** Since v0.4.2 the bottom bar
   (and anything else on the main screen that depends on the time) only changes when the screen
   redraws: opening the app, coming back to it, a tap, or a change. Left open across a boundary,
   it keeps the old state until touched. The main screen should redraw itself at the next time
   something on it is due to change, including:
   - 5:00 PM (daytime → evening bar) and 4:00 AM;
   - Start wind-down now appearing (its window before go to bed), wind-down, go to bed (Time for
     bed);
   - the morning windows opening and closing (I'm up early, Back to bed, Not ready for bed);
   - an alarm's time passing, and the morning being over (the rings going);
   - Undo's moment passing.

4. **Redo the README with new screenshots and GIFs.** The README's pictures (`docs/images/`) were

   bar, Settings split into pages (Alarms, Mornings, Home, Notifications, Wind-down, Shortcuts),

5. **Weekly summary: this week so far, and earlier weeks.** Today the summary screen ("Your
   week") only shows the last summary Home Assistant sent, which covers the 7 days before it was
   sent (a rolling 7 days, not tied to the setting).
   - Weeks are cut at the **day and time in Settings → Weekly summary** (default Sunday 6:00 PM):
     a week runs from that moment to the same moment 7 days later.
   - Opening the summary shows **the current week so far** (since the last cut-off, up to now),
     fresh each time it's opened.
   - **Earlier weeks:** step back (and forward again) through previous weeks, each one shown as
     it was at its cut-off.
   - The weekly notification still goes out at the cut-off and opens the week that just ended.
   - **How far back: 8 weeks** (decided 30 Oct). Home Assistant already keeps 60 days of history
     (`KEEP_DAYS` in `weekly.py`), which covers it; no change there.
