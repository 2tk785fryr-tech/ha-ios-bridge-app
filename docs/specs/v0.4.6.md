## Items

1. **Ending a nap leaves the nap on screen.** Seen 30 Oct: a nap was set (everything shown was
   right), then cancelled. The alarms were cancelled, but the main screen still showed the nap
   and the bottom bar still offered End nap, until the app was force-closed and reopened.
   Ending a nap (End nap, its last alarm stopped) should clear the Napping card and the bar's nap
   state straight away.
   - Cause found (not built yet): the main screen only redraws on state it tracks, and the nap's
     alarms aren't part of it. A fix is set aside (git stash, "v0.4.6 item 1 fix") until the list
     is final.

2. **"I'm awake" on mornings with no alarms, by hand and automatically.** Seen 30 Oct (Friday):
   no alarms, Sleep In on. Getting up, there was no good way to wake the house and the phone;
   Good Morning - App had to be run by hand. It ended Sleep Focus, made the announcement and
   turned Awake For Day on, as it should, but **Sleep In stayed on** (problem).
   - Cause found (read-only): on alarm mornings Sleep In is handled by the stop flow; on a
     no-alarm morning nothing turns it off. Good Morning - App doesn't touch it, and the
     integration only turns it off on undo bedtime.
   - **By hand:** "I'm awake" in the app on mornings with no alarms (bottom bar, ⋯, a Siri phrase,
     a Lock Screen / Action button control). It tells Home Assistant, which turns Sleep In off,
     Awake For Day on, disarms, and sends RUN_GOOD_MORNING so Good Morning - App runs.
   - **Automatically:** Michael's idea: out of bed → bedroom door opens → down the hall (hallway
     presence) → push to run Good Morning. The risk: a glass of water, letting the dog out, or
     Seth still in bed (he wouldn't want the bedroom curtains opening). **To decide** (options in
     the reply of 30 Oct, below).
   - Entities read back 30 Oct: bed `binary_sensor.master_bedroom_bed_presence_bed_occupied_left`
     (Michael) and `…_right` (Seth); bedroom door `binary_sensor.aqara_door_and_window_sensor_door_2`;
     hallway `binary_sensor.hallway_master_presence`, `binary_sensor.aqara_presence_multisensor_fp300_occupancy`,
     `binary_sensor.upstairs_occupancy`.

   **Options for the automatic part (not decided):**
   - a. **Not before a set time** (e.g. 7:00, or when the curtains would open): a 3 AM trip never
     counts.
   - b. **Up for a while, not back in bed:** out of bed for 15–20 min without getting back in. A
     water or dog trip is short and ends back in bed.
   - c. **The path:** out of bed → bedroom door → hallway presence (Michael's idea), as the trigger
     that starts the wait in b.
   - d. **Phone off the charger:** the companion app's battery state going from Charging to Not
     Charging is a strong sign you're up for the day.
   - e. **Ask first:** an actionable push "Up for the day?" with *Good morning* / *Back to bed*;
     no answer and still up after 10 more minutes → it runs.
   - f. **Seth still in bed** (right side occupied): a quiet good morning: everything except the
     bedroom curtains and the Master Bedroom announcement.
   - g. **Two steps:** the harmless part at once (disarm, Awake For Day, hallway lights), the
     curtains and announcement only once it's sure (b, d or e).

   **Letting the dog out (added 30 Oct):** that means opening the patio door
   (`binary_sensor.aqara_door_and_window_sensor_door_4`, Dinette Patio Door), so the house alarm
   has to be off *before* the door opens, even when it isn't the morning yet. The alarm is ecobee,
   run from the phone (`RUN_DISARM` → Set Disarm, `RUN_ARM_STAY` → Set Arm Stay), so a disarm
   takes a few seconds to reach the phone and run.
   - h. **Night trip:** on the path (out of bed → bedroom door → hallway) at any hour, disarm at
     once; back in bed for a few minutes → re-arm. The integration already does exactly this
     after a temporary alarm (re-arm once back in bed for *Re-arm after back in bed for*). If the
     trip turns into the morning (b, d or e), it stays disarmed and good morning runs.
   - i. **"Let the dog out"** by hand: a Lock Screen / Action button control or Siri phrase that
     disarms now and re-arms once you're back in bed. For when you'd rather not have the house
     disarm on its own at night.
   - Either way, opening the patio door before the disarm has run would set the alarm off; h
     disarms while you're still in the hallway, which should be well before you reach the door.

## Open from earlier (not decided; move into Items to build)

- **The bottom bar switches from daytime to evening (5 PM) only on the next screen update**, not
  on the clock alone (since v0.4.2).
- **Critical alerts from the app's own notifications** need a separate entitlement request to
  Apple (developer.apple.com → Contact → Critical Alerts entitlement). Today Critical is only
  offered for the notifications Home Assistant sends.
- **Home Assistant down overnight while you're home**, with drive checks in Home Assistant: the
  app skips its own leave reminders at home, so that morning gets none. Could keep one phone
  reminder at leave time as a backup.
- **Not yet seen working for real:** Live Activity pushes (needs 0.4.5 and the key in the
  options), Apple Weather forecasts, drive checks in Home Assistant, the new widget sizes,
  wind-down on a real evening.
- **Backstop cutover** after soak mornings 2 and 3 (`docs/ha-handoffs-v0.4.1.md`, section 6).
