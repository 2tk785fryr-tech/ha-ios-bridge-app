# Bedtime Bridge v0.4.10

Started 1 Nov 2026. App and integration (0.4.10; 0.4.9 is the last released integration). The
bugs and rough edges from testing on 31 Oct and 1 Nov, before the big change in 0.5.0. Items
1–13 built 1 Nov 2026 (hand-offs: `docs/ha-handoffs-v0.4.10.md`). Items added after aren't built
until Michael says to build.

Sorted from the 16 reports in `bedtime_bridge_reports/reports.jsonl` (31 Oct 15:59 to 1 Nov
08:28). Each item names the reports it comes from by time.

## Rules (as for v0.4.9)

- Pull first; work on `claude/ios-home-assistant-app-73ao17`; commit in small steps and push;
  Michael merges from GitHub.
- Build exactly what the list says; if something can't work as written, stop and ask.
- Bump `custom_components/bedtime_bridge/manifest.json` to 0.4.10 if the integration changes. A
  test for every item (pytest `tests/ha`, `BedtimeBridgeTests`); run both, and build and run on
  the iPhone before an item is done.
- Don't change the live Home Assistant: HA-side changes go in `docs/ha-handoffs-v0.4.10.md`, phone
  shortcuts in `docs/shortcuts.md`. Update `docs/setup.md` with any new step, and the README (and
  its pictures) where a screen changes.
- Protected actions use the bottom-bar slide-and-hold.

## Items

### The alarms

1. **Stop on the banner made a 4th alarm next to the ones already set.** 1 Nov (08:17, 08:21),
   own alarms 8:00, 8:10, 8:20. From the activity log:
   - 08:00 Snooze on the 8:00 → rings again 08:09.
   - 08:09 Stop on the banner of the snoozed 8:00 → v0.4.8 item 4 turned it into a snooze by
     setting a **new alarm at 08:18**, and the Stop screen opened (Keep snoozing / Slide to stop /
     Plans changed?). Michael chose Keep snoozing.
   - 08:10 the 8:10 rang → Snooze → rings again 08:19.
   - So 08:18, 08:19 and 08:20 were all set, and it wasn't clear which alarm was which.

   The change:
   - **No extra alarm when the series already has one coming.** Stop on the banner only sets a
     snooze alarm when no alarm in this wake-up (set or snoozing) rings before the snooze would.
     Otherwise it's a snooze onto that alarm: nothing new is set, and the Stop screen says
     "Next: the 8:10 alarm, in 1 min".
   - **The Stop screen names the alarm it's about** by its set time ("The 8:00 alarm · 2nd snooze")
     and lists what's left in this wake-up with their ring times, snoozed ones as
     "8:10 → rings 8:19".
   - Test: the 1 Nov sequence above leaves 3 alarms, not 4; a banner Stop with no later alarm
     within the snooze still sets one.

2. **Stop on the banner ended every alarm and ran good morning, with alarms still set.** 1 Nov
   08:18 (report 08:21): the 08:18 alarm from item 1 rang and Michael pressed Stop on the banner.
   Home Assistant said he was up, so v0.4.8's "already up → a real stop" ran, and the stop
   behavior (*Keep the rest until I'm up*) cancelled the rest: the 8:19 snooze and the 8:20
   alarm went, `stopped` with no alarms left, RUN_GOOD_MORNING ran.
   - Why "up" (history, read 1 Nov): Michael's side of the bed
     (`binary_sensor.master_bedroom_bed_presence_bed_occupied_left`) went off at 08:17:14, 66 s
     before the Stop, past the app's "bed empty over 1 min". It had also read off until 07:44
     while he was filing reports from bed. The bed needs adjusting and the sensors calibrating
     (Michael, not the app); after this item a wrong reading can't end the alarms anyway.

   The change: **Stop on the banner never ends anything but that ring.** Whatever Home Assistant
   says, a series alarm's banner Stop is a snooze (item 1) and opens the Stop screen. Ending
   takes the slide-and-hold:
   - **Slide to stop:** that alarm only (and its snooze). The rest of the wake-up stays.
   - **Hold at the end:** every alarm left in this wake-up.
   - Home Assistant still runs good morning on the first alarm stopped (the slide or the hold),
     as before (Michael, 1 Nov). It's sent again on a later stop only when the shortcut never
     reported it ran. A banner Stop is a snooze, so it never does.
   - Test: a banner Stop with Home Assistant saying up leaves the other alarms and sends
     `snoozed`, not `stopped`; the slide stops one; the hold ends all and sends `stopped`; good
     morning runs on the first stop, once.

3. **How a ring ends (decided 1 Nov).** Same reports: half asleep, Stop on the banner silenced
   the alarm and ended it. Michael's rule, which items 1 and 2 follow (he may rethink how it all
   works later):
   - **Snooze** silences the ring and snoozes, as now.
   - **Stop** silences the ring but **doesn't end the alarm**. The alarm is held as a snooze
     (rings again after the snooze length, or the next alarm if that's sooner, item 1) and the
     app opens the Stop screen. If iOS won't open the app from the banner, the "Snoozed · slide
     to end" notification opens it, as in v0.4.8.
   - **The Stop screen** is the only place a ring ends:
     - *Slide to end this alarm:* that alarm only; the rest of the wake-up stays.
     - *Hold at the end:* every alarm left in this wake-up.
     - *Keep snoozing* and *Plans changed?* as now.
     - Do nothing and it rings again.
   - **Nothing else ends a ring:** not Home Assistant saying you're up (item 2), and not a
     setting. **Settings → Alarms → Stop behavior** (*Keep the rest until I'm up* / *Cancel the
     rest* / *Keep them all*) is removed, and a saved value is ignored.
   - No 1-minute re-ring: the snooze is what wakes you again.
   - Test: Stop sends `snoozed` and leaves every alarm; only the slide or the hold sends
     `stopped`; the setting is gone from Settings and from `BridgeSettings`' decoding of old
     values.

4. **"Plan changed: update alarms" in the up-early window.** Automatic report, 31 Oct 15:59
   (`replan_in_up_early`), during the clock test with alarms set for 22:00. The re-plan prompt
   shouldn't show while this morning's alarms can be ended early.

### Reports

13. **Edit or delete a report after sending it.** 1 Nov 07:07 (editing), and Michael asked the same
    day for deleting. Neither exists today. Testing → Reports sent lists the last 20 from this
    phone.
    - **Edit:** tapping one edits its note; saving sends it again with `edits: <id>`, and the
      integration replaces that line in `reports.jsonl` (keeping `received`, adding `edited`).
    - **Delete:** swipe or *Delete report*, with a confirm. The app sends `deletes: <id>`; the
      integration removes the line and its screenshot. Automatic reports from the app or Home
      Assistant can't be deleted from the phone.
    - Test (pytest): an edit replaces the line, not appends; an unknown id appends as new; a
      delete removes the line and the PNG; an unknown id changes nothing.
