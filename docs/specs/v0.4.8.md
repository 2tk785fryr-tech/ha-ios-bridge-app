## Items

1. **Bedtime didn't start after setting your own alarms late (30–31 Oct).** Home late, alarms set
   in the app downstairs at 3:52 AM (own time, 10:00 AM; Sleep In went on, correctly). In bed at
   4:01, but the bedtime nudge never sent RUN_BEDTIME, so the lights stayed on.
   - **Cause (read-only, Bedtime Nudge trace 04:04 AM):** your Bedtime Nudge blueprint
     (`bedtime_nudge_v1-02.yaml`) only nudges inside a clock window: with no shift it's the off-day
     window, 21:00–04:00. In bed at 4:01, the 3-minute dwell ended at 4:04, outside the window, so
     it stopped at "Inside the widest bedtime window: false". Nothing in Bedtime Bridge was involved.
   - **Fix (proposal, to decide):** when Bedtime Bridge has alarms set for later and bedtime hasn't
     run since they were set, getting into bed is bedtime at any hour, until a set time before the
     first alarm (e.g. 2 h). The integration adds a `binary_sensor.bedtime_bridge_bedtime_pending`
     (on from a plan or own alarms until bedtime runs or that cut-off), and the blueprint nudges when
     it's on even outside its window. The blueprint change is a hand-off (it's your blueprint in
     Home Assistant, not in this repo). Alternative: just widen the off-day window's end (e.g.
     06:00).

2. **The bedtime shortcut asked for a wake-up time although your own alarms were set, and cleared
   them.** When you ran the bedtime shortcut by hand, it asked "what time do you want to get up?";
   you cancelled, ran it again and picked the same time, which set the alarms again.
   - **Cause (read-only):** the shortcut's first step, *Plan Wake-Up Alarms*, plans from the
     calendar. On a day off it finds no shift or event, so it returns *Has Alarm: false* and, with
     *Set Alarms* on, clears the app's alarms (as it's meant to for a "no alarm" night). Your own
     alarms from *Set alarms for a time* went with them, and the shortcut then asked the day-off
     question.
   - **Fix:** when your own alarms are set for the coming morning, *Plan Wake-Up Alarms* keeps them
     and returns them (*Has Alarm: true*, the times, "Your own time"), so the shortcut doesn't ask
     and nothing is cleared.
   - **To decide:** if it does find a shift or event for that same morning: keep your own alarms,
     or the earlier wake-up of the two wins (as an appointment and a shift do today)?

3. **The 2 hours before the alarms: End alarms belongs in the bar, with Sleep later.** In the up-early
   window (2 h before the first alarm) the *End this morning's alarms* card was on the main screen;
   it should move into the bottom bar, which should also offer *Sleep later*.
   - The bar is meant to show "1 alarm set · first at 10:00" with *I'm up early* and *Sleep later*
     in that window; the card shows as well. To confirm with Michael: what did the bar say?
   - **Fix:** in that window the card leaves the screen and the bar is the one place: *I'm up
     early* (slide-and-hold) and *Sleep later*.

4. **Ending an alarm from the banner is too easy.** Using the phone when the alarm went off, it
   showed as a banner with *Snooze* and *Stop*; one tap on Stop ended it and Good Morning ran.
   - **What's possible (AlarmKit):** the banner and Lock Screen buttons are the system's: Stop
     always stops, there's no hold-to-stop there. What the app can do:
     a. **Stop from the banner counts as a snooze** unless you're up: the alarm comes back after
        the snooze length unless you end it with the slide-and-hold in the app, or Home Assistant
        sees you're up (bedroom door / bed empty, as *Keep the rest until I'm up* already does).
     b. **The second button opens the app's Stop screen** (slide-and-hold) instead of being a plain
        Snooze; the first stays the system's Stop (with a.).
     c. **Good Morning waits until you're up:** stopping an alarm in bed no longer runs Good Morning
        straight away; it runs once Home Assistant sees you up (or the app's slide-and-hold ends the
        morning).
   - **To decide:** which of a–c.
