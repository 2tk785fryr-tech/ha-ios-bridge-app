# Bedtime Bridge v0.4.1 spec

Status: agreed 28 Oct 2026, in progress. Integration version: **0.4.1**.

Order: item 1 first, then 2–5 and 19, then the rest. Item 15 is a procedure, not code. No manual
kill switch for the backstops (decided). HA-side changes go in `docs/ha-handoffs-v0.4.1.md`.

## Evidence (28 Oct 2026, from Home Assistant's logs)

Alarms 4:20/4:25/4:30 (Days, 6:30 start, leave-by 5:43). 4:20 snoozed, 4:25 snoozed, 4:29 stopped
(Wake Mike + Good Morning ran, Awake For Day on), 4:30 snoozed, 4:34 snoozed. 4:35 "change this
morning" +30 min, reason Tired, from the snooze screen: app sent alarm_times 4:40/4:50/5:00, phase
"snoozing". HA only turned wake_fade off (already off): no bedtime routine, no RUN_BACK_TO_BED.
Michael ran Back To Bed by hand and turned the lights off by hand. 4:40 rang and was snoozed; 4:41
stopped and the app reported NO alarms left; 4:50 and 5:00 never rang. Bed occupied until 4:56,
exit latch off the whole time. Wake Confirm nudged at 4:51; the live Backstop's early rung fired at
4:55 (old wake_rung_* times). Leave-by moved to 6:13 though the shift still started at 6:30. The
app showed "alarms pending".

## 1. CRITICAL: after "change this morning", only the first new alarm rang

- Find why 4:50 and 5:00 were lost and the 4:41 stop reported none left (replaceAlarms after a
  change, the snoozing alarm stopped just before, the stop path, series/kept bookkeeping).
  Michael was in bed, so "are you up?" should have kept them.
- After any change or edit, read back what AlarmKit actually has and send THOSE times to Home
  Assistant. If fewer are set than planned, show an error and report it to HA.

## 2. Back to bed took the "snoozing" path after an alarm had been stopped

Nothing in the house went back to bed.
- App: send phase "up" whenever any alarm in this wake-up was stopped (or the wake routine ran),
  wherever the change is started. The snooze screen currently always sends "snoozing".
- Integration: don't trust the phase alone. If routine_ran or Awake For Day is on, use the "up"
  path: Sleep In, then the bedtime routine (lights off, Awake For Day off, curtain), then
  RUN_BACK_TO_BED.
- Test: stop, then snooze, then change from the snooze screen → bedtime routine on and
  RUN_BACK_TO_BED sent.

## 3. The new series uses the morning's own pattern, not the general settings

- Days is 3 alarms, 5 min apart, wake = last: +30 should be 4:50/4:55/5:00, not 4:40/4:50/5:00.
  MorningChange.make uses settings.alarmCount/alarmSpacingMinutes/wakeTimeIs; use the rule that
  built this morning's series (shift rule count, spacing, wakeIs), for every choice including
  "pick a time".

## 4. Replace "alarms pending" with the real status

- It showed right after the new alarms were set and read as "not set". Show what AlarmKit has: "3
  alarms set · 4:50 4:55 5:00", or an error "Couldn't set 2 of 3 alarms". Tonight, the Stop screen
  and the widget update immediately after any change.

## 5. "Snoozes last morning" undercounted (read 1; the morning had 5)

- Count the whole morning across a change; reset only when the morning is over (the series after
  the last change ends, or the next plan).

## 6. Confirm screen: which backstops still fire at their old times

Until the cutover (item 15), the confirm screen says which backstop times will still fire at their
old times (the house backstop reads sensor.wake_rung_*) and which move.

## 7. "Change this morning" chooses: shift the alarms only, or the entire wake-up

- **Shift alarms only** (sleep longer, same start): only the alarms move. Leave-by, the leave-by
  reminder, the widget's leave-by, and the deadline and late backstops stay. The EARLY backstop
  moves to N min after the new last alarm if it would otherwise fall before it (N = setting,
  default 5).
- Hard limit for Shift alarms only: the new LAST alarm may never be after the late backstop.
  Choices that would pass it are disabled with the reason and an offer to switch to Shift entire
  wake-up; "pick a time" stops at the limit. Between deadline and late: allowed with a warning.
  The integration refuses a scope "alarms" change past the late backstop (logged, nothing moves)
  and the app shows the error.
- **Shift entire wake-up** (start is later, e.g. a snow delay): alarms, leave-by, reminder, widget
  and all three backstops move by the same amount. No limit; being later than the calendar event
  is fine.
- Settings → Change this morning: the reasons list (add, rename, remove, reorder), and per reason
  "Shift alarms only" / "Shift entire wake-up" / "Ask". Starting defaults: Snow delay and
  Postponed → entire wake-up; Tired → alarms only; Not feeling well, Canceled, Other and new
  reasons → Ask. Also the early-backstop gap N (default 5 min). "Ask" shows both choices; the
  confirm screen always says which one is happening.
- morning_changed gets "scope": "alarms" | "wake_up"; the integration moves the whole ladder only
  for "wake_up".

## 8. Show the three backstops in "Your morning" on the main screen

- In time order: Wake up · Early backstop · Deadline backstop · Late backstop · Leave by · Arrive ·
  start. Backstop steps in their own small style (shield dot, not bold).
- Times from Home Assistant's ladder; if HA is unreachable, computed from leave-by and the offsets
  and marked "estimate". A status line: "Backstops armed", the idle reason, or "Backstop fault:
  <reason>"; "First day back +15 min" when it applies. Updates right after any change,
  weather/traffic move or re-plan.
- New integration option "House backstop reads: Bedtime Bridge ladder / old template", default old
  template until the cutover. While old: the integration also reports the sensor.wake_rung_* times
  and the timeline shows THOSE (they're what fires), noted "House backstop: old times until
  cutover".

## 9. Plain words instead of "alarm_cause → Days"

Replace the debug line "alarm_cause → Days" at the bottom of the main screen with "Reason for
alarm: Days shift / Swings shift / Appointment: <title> / Your own time / No alarm" (+ " (morning
changed: <reason>)"). App display only; values sent to HA stay. Check the app for any other raw
keys or snake_case shown to Michael.

## 10. Hide "Set 3 alarms, up by …" once the alarms are set

- When isSet(plan): show the status line (item 4) instead of the big button.
- Bring the button back, reworded, only when something changed: calendar changed ("Calendar
  changed: re-plan alarms"), override or a plan setting changed ("Plan changed: update alarms"),
  or alarms missing ("Set the missing alarms").
- After a change this morning or an edit, count the alarms as set; never show a button that would
  undo the change. Show "Changed this morning: <reason>".

## 11. A "wake up" appointment on a work morning replaces the shift's alarms (going in late)

- Today the earliest wake-up wins, so a 7:00 marked appointment is ignored on a Days morning.
- Not marked: the shift's alarms. Marked and starting before the shift ends: it replaces the shift
  for that morning (its own leave-by, drive, weather, get-ready), earlier or later. Marked but
  after the shift ends: the shift, with a warning.
- Main screen, in Preview and after setting: "Using Ultrasound at 7:00 AM for your alarms instead
  of your Days shift (6:30). You marked it wake up, so you're going in late." With an unmarked
  appointment that morning: "Ultrasound at 10:00 AM isn't marked wake up, so your Days alarms are
  used." Reason line: "Appointment: Ultrasound (instead of Days)".
- HA: ladder block type "appointment" with its leave-by; override stays Auto; add
  "replaces_shift". Until the cutover, note that the house backstop still uses the shift's times.

## 12. "Sleep later" from Tonight, before bed (e.g. a late night before Swings)

- +30 min, +1 hr, +2 hr, "8 hours from now", or a time. Works like Shift alarms only (morning's
  own pattern; leave-by and deadline/late backstops stay; early follows).
- Limit: last alarm not after the late backstop; for a morning with no backstops (Swings), not
  after leave-by minus get-ready time.
- One slide-and-hold (5 s), no Face ID. Reason optional. HA gets morning_changed, scope "alarms",
  phase "bedtime"; nothing in the house changes; Sleep In follows the first alarm. "Back to planned
  alarms" until the first alarm rings.

## 13. Edit, add or remove single alarms after they're set

Tonight lists them: alarms still ahead, and a snoozing alarm at its snooze-until time (added 28 Oct:
moving a snoozing alarm later keeps the number of alarms; leaving it unchanged keeps it snoozing;
removing it ends it). Every edit is Shift alarms only: late and deadline
backstops never move, no alarm after the late backstop, early follows the last alarm. HA gets the
new alarm_times (changed_by "edit"). Counts as set for item 10.

## 14. Your own alarm on a day off or leave: backstops off by default, with a switch

- Ladder type "own", off unless turned on (switch where the alarm is set: day-off prompt, Tonight,
  Change this morning, item 18).
- Settings: default on/off for day off and for leave (both off); offsets from the LAST alarm:
  early +15, deadline +30, late +45 (editable, validated).
- Firing gentle by default (push and early light ramp; no critical sound, no speakers), setting to
  use the full actions. Only takes effect after the cutover (the old backstop fires on shifts
  only); until then the switch says so.
- HA: ladder block carries type "own", on/off and offsets; status idle_own_off when off.

## 15. Backstop cutover (procedure)

a. Ship v0.4.1 with item 1 fixed first.
b. Set the integration's Work calendar option to calendar.calendar.
c. Soak 3 normal Days mornings: integration ladder vs sensor.wake_rung_* within a few minutes (real
   drive time), 15 min earlier on a first day back, status "armed". Any missing/fault/unexplained
   >10 min difference restarts the soak.
d. HA chat applies the hand-offs: Watchdog and Readout v1-30 first (only once v0.4.1 is installed,
   since they treat a standing "done" as stuck), then the Backstop's rung inputs →
   sensor.bedtime_bridge_safety_net_*, gated on ladder status armed (for the right "morning") and
   presence. Read every entity id back first.
e. Flip "House backstop reads" (item 8) to Bedtime Bridge ladder; the interim notes in items 6, 8,
   11 and 14 disappear.
f. Watch 3 mornings after. Rollback: inputs back to sensor.wake_rung_*, option back.

v0.4.1 must ship with the interim notes working.

## 16. Settings → Appointments: make the wake-up keyword explain itself

- Rename "Notes contain" to "Wake-up keyword", with: "Put this word or phrase in an event's notes
  and the app will wake you for that event. Events without it are ignored for alarms. Work shifts
  don't need it: they're found by their title (Days, Swings)." Example: "Notes: wake up · bring
  insurance card".
- Say it matches anywhere in the notes, any case; mention setting it per event from Upcoming
  events (link). Show "N upcoming events are marked wake up". Main Settings row: "Wake-up keyword:
  wake up". Update the footer for item 11. Warn if emptied.

## 17. Several leave-by reminders, each with a fresh drive time

- Settings → Drive time → Leave reminders: any of At leave time, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 min before
  (default: 15 min before and at leave time).
- Each re-checks the drive, works out a new leave-by, notifies ("Leave by 5:48 · 32 min drive (5
  min worse than planned)" / "Leave now · 35 min drive"); later reminders follow the new leave-by.
- Mechanism: HA sends a time-sensitive push RUN_LEAVE_CHECK at each time; a Shortcuts automation
  runs a new App Intent "Check drive and remind", which re-checks, notifies and reports leave_by;
  the integration reschedules the rest. Document in docs/shortcuts.md. Bedtime-scheduled local
  notifications remain as a fallback, marked "not re-checked".
- Stop once he has left (presence) or 15 min after leave-by, or if the morning is changed or
  skipped. A new leave-by updates the widget, "Your morning" and the backstops (earlier only).

## 18. "Wake at a different time": rounded default, two steps, preview

- Step 1: time defaults to 6 hours from now rounded UP to the interval (default 15 min: 6:54 →
  7:00, 7:02 → 7:15). Settings: interval Exact / 5 / 10 / 15 / 30 min, and whether the picker
  allows only those intervals or any minute. Under the time: "This time is the first / last
  alarm", number of alarms (default 3), minutes apart (default 10), backstops switch (default off;
  item 14). All defaults editable in Settings. Button "Next".
- Step 2: "Your morning" preview with the alarms and backstops (if on). Tap to change or remove an
  alarm; add one before, after or between; change or remove a backstop. Changing alarms
  recalculates the backstops from the new last alarm and discards backstop edits, with the note
  "Changing the alarms updates the backstops and undoes changes you made to them." "Set alarms"
  sets exactly what's shown, then shows the real status (item 4).
- Used everywhere the sheet is: skipping with your own time, the day-off prompt, Tonight.

## 19. Ladder status "done" must not stand

- Today: after an appointment morning with no covered shift ahead (day off before Swings, leave),
  status stays "done" until the next plan, so it reads "done" at 18:00 and Watchdog v1-30 would
  alert. After a shift morning it reads "done" ~5–20 min (rollover at late + 60 is before the
  shift start, until the next current_shift poll).
- Fix: when the morning ends (rollover, or a recompute finds this morning finished), go straight
  to the next state: armed for the next covered morning, or the matching idle_* reason (no shift /
  uncovered / leave / override off). Don't return "done" from _ladder_morning for a finished
  morning; fall through to the shift branch's status.
- Tests: appointment morning, no covered shift ahead → after late + 60 the status is the idle
  reason, not "done"; shift morning → after late + 60 armed for the next shift (or idle) without
  waiting for the current_shift poll.
- Tell the HA chat when v0.4.1 is installed, so Watchdog v1-30 can treat "done" as stuck.
