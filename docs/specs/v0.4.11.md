## Items

### Your morning (the timeline)

1. **Leave by, worst case.** (Michael, 1 Nov.) On a morning with a drive (a shift, or an
   appointment with a location), Your morning gets one more step: the latest you can leave on a
   bad-traffic day and still get there **when it starts, not early**.
   - **The time:** the event's start minus the worst-case drive (plus any weather minutes already
     added to the drive). No arrival buffer, unlike Leave by, which gets you there early by the
     buffer in settings.
   - **The worst-case drive** is the one the app already works out for the morning Live Activity
     (`MorningActivity.worstCaseMinutes`): Google's worst case when there's a Google key, else the
     longest of the services asked. With only one service and no Google key there is no worst
     case, and the step doesn't show.
   - **Its note**, under the time: *"Leaving now gets you there right when it starts, not early."*
     plus the drive it uses (*"Google worst case 41 min"*).
   - **Its look:** a ring dot (outline) in a new deep blue, `Theme.leaveByWorst`, darker than the
     light blue of Leave by (`Theme.leaveBy`, #60A5FA) and still visible on the midnight ground;
     something like #3B6FE8, to check on the iPhone. Title *"Leave by · worst case"*, not bold, so
     Leave by stays the main step.
   - **Where it sits:** in time order with the other steps. It's usually after Leave by and before
     Arrive; on a morning where the worst case is much longer than the usual drive it can come
     before Leave by.
   - **Not shown when Leave by already is the worst case:** with *Drive time → Use: Google* and
     *Google plans for: Worst case* in Settings, Leave by is planned on Google's worst case, so the
     extra step is left out.
   - **Not shown when another drive time is longer than Google's worst case** (Michael, 1 Nov):
     if Apple, TomTom or Google's typical day says more minutes than Google's worst case, the worst
     case isn't the worst, so the step is left out. (Without a Google key the worst case is
     already the longest service, so this only applies with Google.)
   - **Not shown** with no location (Be ready instead of a drive) or when the drive time fell back
     to the assumed minutes.
   - Tests: the time is start minus the worst drive (and weather), with no buffer; no step with
     one service; no step with Google + worst case set; no step when another service is longer than Google's worst case; order when it falls before Leave by.

### The bottom bar

2. **Set alarms during or after wind-down asks whether to start bedtime too.** (Michael, 1 Nov.)
   Today Set alarms only sets tomorrow's alarms. Bedtime runs later, from Good night in the bar or
   from getting into bed while bedtime is pending.
   - **When it asks:** from the start of tonight's wind-down (status *winding down* or *bedtime*
     in `WindDownState`) until bedtime runs (`bedtimeRanAt` after wind-down started, or
     `bedtimeRan`). Any other time Set alarms works as now, with no question.
   - **The bubble**, after the preview's Set: a small confirm with two choices:
     - **Set alarms only**: as now. Good night shows in the bar, and getting into bed still
       counts as bedtime.
     - **Set alarms and start bedtime**: sets the alarms, then sends Good night (`RUN_BEDTIME`
       through Home Assistant), the same as tapping Good night in the bar.
     - Dismissing the bubble without a choice sets nothing, like Cancel.
   - **Also for Pick a time** (Set alarms for a time) in the same window, after its time is
     chosen.
   - **Not asked** when wind-down is off in Settings (there's no wind-down window), when you're
     away from home (Home Assistant doesn't run bedtime then), or without the integration.
   - Tests: asks during winding down and bedtime status; not before wind-down, not after bedtime
     ran, not with wind-down off or away; the second choice sends Good night after the alarms are
     set; the first sends nothing.

### Your morning, after the alarms are set

3. **The full timeline stays after the alarms are set.** (Michael, 1 Nov.) Today the full
   Your morning (drive, Leave by, Arrive, the event) shows only while a preview is on screen. Once
   the app has been closed, the plan it set (`lastAppliedPlan`) is gone, because it's kept only in
   memory. Reopening it, say in the middle of the night, shows just the alarms and backstops.
   - **Keep the set plan:** the plan the alarms were set from is saved (UserDefaults, like the
     alarm series) with the time it was worked out. It's kept through the morning (below), and
     cleared when the alarms are cleared some other way (No alarms, a skip, Clear).
   - **Show it:** while those alarms are set, Your morning is drawn from the saved plan: the same
     full timeline as the preview (wind-down and bed, wake, get ready, backstops, Leave by,
     item 1's worst case, Arrive, the event), with the alarms as they're set now (snoozes,
     temporary alarms, edits).
   - **No new requests:** opening the app doesn't ask for drive times, weather or the calendar to
     redraw it. The saved plan is replaced only when the alarms are set again (Preview and Set, a
     re-plan, the bedtime shortcut's plan, a morning change).
   - **The footer**, at the bottom of the card: *"As of Sun 10:42 PM · tap Preview to update"*
     (the day only when it isn't today). Preview works as now: a fresh plan, which replaces what's
     shown once it's set.
   - **After an alarm is stopped** (Michael, 1 Nov): the backstops, Leave by and the event are
     still wanted, so the timeline stays, and the saved plan isn't cleared by ending the alarms.
     - The alarms you stopped stay on it, greyed, with *"Stopped 6:12"*; snoozes as they are
       (*"Snoozing · rings 6:21"*); alarms ended without ringing say *"Ended"*.
     - Steps already past are dimmed; the next one still to come is highlighted (*Next*), e.g.
       Leave by once you're up.
     - The backstops show as now (fired or still to come), with Leave by, item 1's worst case,
       Arrive and the event.
     - It stays until the event starts or the morning is over (the late backstop + 60 min, as the
       rings stay today), whichever is later; then the card goes as now.
     - The footer stays (*"As of 10:42 PM · tap Preview to update"*). A morning change or
       Plans changed? replaces the plan, as a set does.
   - **Without a saved plan** (alarms set by an older version, or your own alarms with no drive):
     the alarms-only timeline as now.
   - Tests: the plan survives a restart and is cleared by No alarms, a skip or Clear; the timeline draws from it
     with no planner call; the footer's text, with and without the day; a new set replaces it; after a stop the plan stays, stopped alarms read Stopped, the next step is marked, and it goes once the event starts or the morning is over.

4. **An evening preview stays too.** (Michael, 1 Nov.) Previewing at 6 PM to see tonight and
   tomorrow means previewing again an hour later, because the preview is gone once you leave the
   screen or the app closes. Like item 3, the last preview is saved and shown until it's replaced.
   - **Saved:** the plan from Preview (or Pick a time's preview), with the time it was worked
     out. Shown the same way as now (*Preview · not set* on the top card, the full Your morning),
     with item 3's footer: *"As of 6:02 PM · tap Preview to update"*.
   - **Refreshed for free by the plan check:** Home Assistant already sends `RUN_PLAN_PREVIEW`
     2 h before wind-down (6 PM with only an appointment), and *Preview Tomorrow* plans in the
     background without setting anything. That plan is saved as the preview, whether or not you
     previewed earlier, so at 6 PM it's already on screen when you open the app; no request is
     added. Tap Preview only for newer information. Wind-down starting doesn't fetch again (it's at most 2 h after the plan check).
   - **Replaced or cleared when:**
     - the alarms are set: the set plan (item 3) takes over;
     - bedtime runs: the bedtime shortcut sets the alarms from a fresh plan, which takes over;
     - you Preview again;
     - No alarms or a skip for that morning;
     - the morning it was for has passed.
   - **The calendar changing** after the preview: the existing *Calendar changed · re-plan* line
     shows in the bar as it does for set alarms, and the saved preview stays until you re-plan.
   - Set alarms works as now: it plans fresh before setting, so it never sets from an old preview.
   - Tests: a preview survives a restart; the plan check's preview replaces it; set, bedtime, No
     alarms and the next day clear or replace it; the footer reads the preview's time.
