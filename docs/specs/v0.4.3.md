slide-and-hold from v0.4.2 item 6. Signed shortcut files stay out of git.

## 1. A manual "Plan and set alarms" asks first

- Today: one tap plans and sets the alarms immediately, no preview, no confirmation.
- Pressed by hand (bottom bar, ⋯, quick actions, anywhere in the app): show a preview first
  (alarm times, reason, morning, backstops) with [Set alarms] and Cancel. Plain button, no
  slide-and-hold (it only adds alarms).
- Bedtime (bed sensor → bedtime shortcut → plan) and the Shortcuts "Plan Wake Alarms" action stay

## 2. Undo after a manual set, until bedtime runs

- After [Set alarms] from item 1, the bottom bar's status row reads "3 alarms set · Undo".
- Undo restores exactly what was there before (previous alarms, or none), sends Home Assistant the
  matching message, Sleep In follows. Protected (slide-and-hold).
- Available until bedtime runs that night or the alarms are changed another way.

## 3. "No alarms tomorrow", any time before the morning

- Today "No alarms" only exists during the morning; the night before there's only "Skip", meant
  for a shift still on the calendar.
- Clears tomorrow's alarms (temporary alarms stay), with a Sleep In switch on its confirm (default
  on). In ⋯ and as a bottom-bar button in the "alarms set, before bed" state; protected.
- Home Assistant: cancelled message with sleep_in. Ladder follows the calendar: no shift → idle;
  shift still on the calendar → backstops stay on, and the confirm says so ("Days at 6:30 is still
  on your calendar, so the backstops stay on. Delete the shift or use Skip if you're not
  working.").
- Undo until bedtime runs (as item 2).

## 4. When the calendar changes after the alarms are set, offer to fix it; never change alarms silently

- Seen 29 Oct: tomorrow's Days shift was deleted after the 4:20 alarms were set; the alarms
  stayed; Home Assistant's plan_out_of_date turned on but the app offered nothing.
- On a calendar change (EventKit notification, and HA's plan_out_of_date when the app opens or
  plans): notification "Tomorrow changed: Days shift deleted. Your 4:20 alarms are still set.
  Re-plan?" with a Re-plan action, and "Calendar changed · re-plan" in the bottom bar.
- Re-plan opens the item 1 preview with the new result ("No alarm needed") and [Set alarms] /
  [Clear alarms]; nothing changes until confirmed. Never removes or moves alarms on its own (a
  sync glitch could make a shift look deleted).

## 5. Dynamic wind-down from tomorrow's first alarm

### Times
- Go to bed = tomorrow's FIRST alarm − sleep goal (default 6.5 h). Wind-down starts = go to bed −
  wind-down length (default 60 min). Example Days, first alarm 4:20: wind-down 8:50 PM, go to bed
  9:50 PM.
- Getting home late (any night): after arriving home, at least 30 min free (default), then at
  least 30 min of wind-down (default):
  - wind-down = later of (go to bed − wind-down length) and (home + free time)
  - go to bed = later of (first alarm − sleep goal) and (wind-down + minimum wind-down)

  Bedtime moves later (less sleep) rather than cutting wind-down below the minimum.
- Which mornings: shift mornings, mornings with a wake-up event, and his own alarm set by hand on
  a day off. No alarm → no wind-down. Temporary and nap alarms ignored.
- Evening changes (Sleep later, edits, calendar, override) move the times; if wind-down has
  started it carries on and one notification gives the new go to bed time.

### Evening plan
- Home Assistant sends RUN_PLAN_PREVIEW (default 2 h before the wind-down it works out from the
  shift calendar; 6:00 PM for mornings only an appointment might decide); a Shortcuts automation
  runs a new App Intent "Preview tomorrow" (background, no alarms set), which reports first alarm,
  cause, event and leave-by. If the app doesn't answer, HA uses the shift calendar alone and the
  wind-down notification adds "(appointments not checked)".
- Opening the app during wind-down shows tomorrow's plan already previewed (not set).

### Notifications and shortcuts
- 5 min before wind-down: warning "Wind-down at 8:50 · bed 9:50" with buttons "15 min later"
  (moves wind-down, not go to bed, never below the minimum) and "Cancel tonight's wind-down" (HA
  actionable notification; the integration handles both).
- At wind-down: RUN_WIND_DOWN; the integration turns on the "Winding down helper" (home only). If
  tomorrow's first alarm comes from an event or moved from its usual time (weather, override,
  change), a notification: "Ultrasound at 7:00 AM tomorrow · leave by 6:15 · first alarm 5:00". A
  normal shift morning gets none.
- Cancel (warning button, app, ⋯): helper off, no go to bed notification or reminders tonight,
  RUN_WIND_DOWN_CANCEL for the phone. Plain confirm in the app.
- Go to bed: CRITICAL notification "Time for bed · first alarm 4:20", no warning, no buttons, no
  phone command (the bedtime shortcut already turns on Sleep Focus, which replaces the Wind Down
  Focus since only one Focus can be on). Skipped if already in bed.
- Not in bed: 10 min after go to bed, if not (bedroom door closed AND bed occupied), a
  time-sensitive (not critical) reminder "Still up · first alarm 4:20", every 10 min, stopping
  when in bed, at bedtime, or after 1 h (6 reminders). "Stop bedtime reminders" on the main
  screen (bottom bar and ⋯) stops them for tonight.
- Into bed early: bedtime before or during wind-down ends it (helper off, nothing more).
- Not ready for bed / undo bedtime: wind-down doesn't restart; only the reminders run, from go to
  bed.
- Away overnight: warning and go to bed notifications still go to the phone; no helper, no
  reminders.

### App

## 7. Settings → Reset: four separate resets

Each with a confirm screen (what it does, what it leaves alone, red button).

a. "Reset alarms and backstops to nothing set" (also slide-and-hold): the app cancels every alarm
   it set (regular, temporary, nap, test), clears tonight's plan, skip, own-time replacement,
   morning change, sleep later, nap, leave-by reminders and fallback notifications, resets the
   widget and alarm card. Home Assistant gets a new "reset" message: clears alarm state (next
   alarm, times, cause → noalarm, snoozing, snooze count, last morning change), the ladder's delay
   and weather moves, pending leave checks and phone commands, turns the napping and winding-down
   helpers off, fires a "reset" event; then the ladder is recalculated from the calendar only
   (armed again if a covered shift is on the calendar; the reset never turns the calendar's
   backstops off). Doesn't touch settings, keys, HA links, override, Sleep In, Awake For Day.
b. "Reset settings to defaults": every changeable app setting; not keys, HA links or alarms
   already set. Nothing sent to HA.
c. "Clear API keys": TomTom and Google keys from the Keychain; drive times fall back to Apple
   Maps.
d. "Clear Home Assistant link": webhook addresses and any token from the Keychain; the warning
   says wake routines, Good Morning and backstops stop getting events until paired again.

## 12. Leave days: configurable titles, add from the app

- Settings: "Leave titles", a list like the shift words (default: Leave). An all-day event whose
  title matches one (trimmed, any case) is leave. Other events during leave work as usual.
- "Add leave" (⋯ and Settings): first and last day → ONE all-day event titled with the first leave
  title, first day through last (end date exclusive handled), in the "Calendar for new events"
  (setting, default the calendar holding the shifts). Confirm shows what's added.
- Hand-off: the HA chat updates current_shift.yaml's leave list to the same titles (today only
  "Leave"); from v0.6 the integration reads them from a matching option.

## 13. Add a wake-up event from the app

- For when it's missing: "Add a wake-up event" (⋯, and on the main screen when tomorrow has
  nothing): title, date (default tomorrow, changeable), start time, length (default 1 h), location
  with address suggestions (as the home address field).
- Always a wake-up event: the wake-up keyword is written into its notes. Saved to the "Calendar
  for new events". Afterwards the item 1 preview opens with the new plan.

## 14. Morning Live Activity

## 15. Weekly summary

## Decisions after receipt

- Item 14 (29 Oct): Live Activity push updates need Apple's push entitlement, which the free team
  can't sign. Home Assistant sends RUN_MORNING_UPDATE hourly and when he leaves home; a new
  "Morning Update - App" shortcut automation runs the app in the background to refresh or end
  the activity.
- Item 13 (29 Oct): "Add a wake-up event" is a circle button at the top of the main screen,
  left of Upcoming events and Settings, always shown (instead of a button only when tomorrow
  has nothing). It stays in ⋯.
- Item 5 (29 Oct): the cancel command is `RUN_CANCEL_WIND_DOWN`, not `RUN_WIND_DOWN_CANCEL`.
  Shortcuts' notification trigger only matches "contains", so an automation for `RUN_WIND_DOWN`
  would also have run on the cancel message.
