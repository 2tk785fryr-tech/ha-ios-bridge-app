## 1. Show which alarm you're stopping

The slide-to-stop screen today says "Snoozing" and never says which alarm.

- Show the alarm's time and place in the series: "4:25 AM · alarm 2 of 3".
- Show what will still ring: "Next: 4:30 AM" (or "Last alarm").
- A temporary alarm says "Temporary alarm".

**Done when:** the Stop screen alone tells you which alarm you're ending and what's left.

## 2. End all alarms for this wake-up

- The normal slide still ends only the ringing alarm.
- Ending all takes a deliberate gesture: slide all the way across, then **hold at the end for 5
  seconds** while a ring fills and the phone ticks (haptics). Letting go early cancels. No Face ID.
- Ends every remaining alarm of this wake-up's series. Temporary alarms and other mornings stay.
- Skips the "are you up?" check (Stop behavior *keep until up*): it's a deliberate choice.
- Home Assistant gets one `stopped` with `all: true`; the morning routine (Wake Mike, Good Morning)
  runs once.
- The backstop times don't change (item 6), so they still fire if you stay in bed.

**Done when:** a sleepy swipe or a short hold never ends the series; only the long gesture does,
and the backstops still fire.

## 3. Change this morning: sleep longer or no alarms

For when plans change after the alarms start: a meeting or brunch canceled, a snow delay.

**Where:**
- While snoozing, from the snooze screen.
- After you're up (alarms stopped, Wake Mike and Good Morning already ran, or the alarms ended
  early with item 8), as **Back to bed** on the Tonight screen.

**Harder than item 2:**
1. The same slide-and-hold (5 s) opens it.
2. Pick: **+30 min, +1 hr, +2 hr, pick a time,** or **no alarms**.
3. A confirm screen lists every change: new alarm times, new backstop times, Sleep In.
4. Confirming takes a second 5-second hold.
5. Face ID with attention required (eyes open); passcode if Face ID fails. (Apps can't demand
   attention themselves: iOS applies it to every Face ID check while *Settings → Face ID &
   Passcode → Require Attention for Face ID* is on, which is the default.)

> **Changed in v0.4.1** (see `v0.4.1.md`): the new series uses the morning's own pattern (item 3);
> the phase is "up" whenever an alarm was stopped or the wake routine ran, and the integration
> checks too (item 2); a change is either **Shift alarms only** (backstops and leave-by stay, early
> follows the alarms, the last alarm never after the late backstop) or **Shift entire wake-up**
> (everything moves), chosen per reason (item 7); the app reads back what AlarmKit set and sends
> those times (item 1).

**Works for every morning, shifts included** (e.g. a 2-hour snow delay). The backstops move by the
same amount as the wake-up: this is the one allowed exception in item 6. The new series uses the
usual alarm count, spacing and "wake time is the first/last alarm" setting.

**No alarms on a shift morning:** the alarms go and Sleep In is set, but the backstops **stay on**
while the calendar still shows the shift. The confirm screen says so: "Your calendar still shows
Days at 6:30, so the backstops stay on. Delete the shift if you're not working." Deleting the shift
from the calendar (or skipping the night before, or override Off) is what turns them off.

**What Home Assistant does** (all new in v0.4; nothing does this today. Recipe from the HA fact
sheet, answer 3):
- **While snoozing** (Awake For Day still off): turn `input_boolean.wake_fade` off. Snooze Fade's
  cancel branch fades both nightstands off in 5 s. Nothing else has changed yet, so nothing else is
  undone.
- **After a stop** (Wake Mike and Good Morning ran, Awake For Day on), in this order:
  1. Set `input_boolean.sleep_in` from the new alarms (below), before anything can move the
     curtains.
  2. Turn `input_boolean.bedtime_routine` on: Awake For Day off, Night Mode re-armed, lights swept,
     bedroom curtain closed.
  3. Push `RUN_BACK_TO_BED` (changed 27 Oct 2026, was `RUN_ARM_STAY`). It runs the phone shortcut
     **Back To Bed - App**: Report Shortcut Ran, `MorningState` = `sleeping` (so the next Good
     Morning runs instead of stopping early), Set Arm Stay when at home, Sleep Focus on. Everything
     else is the integration's job on the HA side. Don't use `RUN_BEDTIME` (it re-runs the whole
     phone bedtime shortcut and plans alarms again). See `docs/shortcuts.md`.
- **Stop what's already running:** Wake Confirm's "still in bed" nudges and a Backstop deadline loop
  in progress. Until v0.5 takes over firing, the integration turns
  `automation.wake_confirm_still_in_bed_after_alarm` and `automation.wake_deadline_backstop` off
  (which cancels their running actions) and straight back on, and clears the `wake_backstop` and
  `wake_confirm` notifications. v0.5 replaces this.
- Sleep In: with new alarms, on if the first is after the curtains open (sunrise + offset, the
  existing rule), off if before. With no alarms, a **Sleep In** switch on the confirm screen,
  default on.
- Clears the snooze count for the old series (item 5).
- `RUN_ARM_STAY` stays as it is: sent only after a temporary alarm (disarm mode) once back in bed.
- **Before building item 3:** the phone shortcuts' actual steps (Good Morning, Arm Stay, Disarm,
  Bedtime) go into `docs/shortcuts.md`, written by the local session that has read them. Describe
  the steps only; no server addresses or home IDs (signed shortcuts stay out of git, see
  `.gitignore`). Anything Good Morning changes on the phone (house alarm, Sleep Focus, anything
  else) needs a way back for "back to bed": a step in the arm-stay shortcut or a new command.

**Reason:** picked from a list (snow delay, canceled, postponed, not feeling well, tired, other)
with an optional note. Logged in HA (event + sensor attribute) for tracking. The list is edited in
the app's Settings.

**Done when:** snoozing or already up, you can move or cancel the alarms; the house goes back to
bedtime; new alarms ring at the new time; the backstops follow (or stay, per above); Sleep In
matches; the reason shows in HA's history.

Today the Lock Screen widget drops "Leave by" as soon as no alarm is left.

- After the last alarm, show "Leave by 7:40 AM" and the event name, for shift and appointment
  mornings.
- Back to the normal display 30 minutes after the leave time. The 30 minutes is a setting.

**Done when:** after stopping the last alarm, the widget shows the leave time until 30 minutes
past it.

## 5. Snooze count per morning

> **Changed in v0.4.1** (item 5): the morning's total counts across a change of this morning;
> it's taken only when the morning is really over.

- **Snooze count** resets to 0 when the wake-up is over: every alarm in the series stopped, ended
  by item 2, or changed by item 3. Today it waits until the next alarm rings.
- New **Snoozes last morning** sensor keeps each morning's total, so HA's history and long-term
  statistics track it.

**Done when:** after the last alarm, Snooze count reads 0 and Snoozes last morning shows that
morning's total.

## 6. Leave-by backstop ladder

Replaces the Home Assistant project's "Leave-by Wake Ladder (Phase 2 spec)". It keeps that spec's
leave-by anchor, fail-loud rule, first-day-back extra, morning lock and cutover plan, and changes
it where the app knows more than HA can see: real traffic- and weather-aware leave-by, appointments,
and deliberate morning changes.

The integration only **publishes** the three times. What happens at each (lights, critical
notification, HomePod, bed and bedroom-exit checks) stays in the Wake Deadline Backstop blueprint
(the HA project's phase 3).

### The ladder

| Rung | Meaning | Before leave-by | Normal (leave 05:45) | First day back |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early | Alarms were set and snoozing is over: get up | 50 min | 04:55 | 04:40 |
| Deadline | Forgot the alarm entirely; still time to get ready | 35 min | 05:10 | 04:55 |
| Late | Last moment to rush-ready | 20 min | 05:25 | 05:10 |

Example: 06:30 Days start, 35 min drive, arrive 10 min early → leave-by 05:45.

- **First day back** (first workday after a day with no shift, or after leave) moves all three 15
  minutes earlier, for the head shave.
- The alarms never feed the ladder: snoozing, stopping and item 2 don't move it.
- Settings in HA, as number entities the app can read: `rung_early_minutes` (50, 10–180),
  `rung_deadline_minutes` (35, 10–180), `rung_late_minutes` (20, 5–120), `first_day_back_minutes`
  (15, 0–60, 0 = off), `fallback_drive_minutes` (35, 0–120); all in steps of 5.
- Early > deadline > late. If the settings cross, keep the last valid ladder and log a warning.

### Which mornings

Every morning with a leave-by:
- **Days shifts** (option `ladder_shift_types`, default `["Days"]`).
- **Appointments with a location**, replacing the old "last alarm, +15, +30" safety-net times
  (which could fire inside a snooze cycle). Same offsets as Days for now.
- Swings stays out unless it ever gets a leave-by.

### Where leave-by comes from

1. **The app's leave-by** when tonight's plan has one for that morning (traffic- and weather-aware,
   shifts and appointments).
2. **Calendar leave-by** = shift start − `fallback_drive_minutes` − arrival buffer, when the app
   never planned, the plan failed, or the plan is for a different morning. Appointments have no
   calendar fallback in HA, so they need a plan.
3. **Sanity limit:** the app's leave-by may be at most **30 minutes later** than the calendar
   figure. Earlier is always allowed. Outside the limit the calendar figure wins and HA logs it.

### First day back

- Worked out by the app from the calendar and sent with the plan.
- With no plan, HA checks its own calendar (option `calendar_entity`).
- If that lookup fails, apply the extra (fail early, not late).

> **Changed in v0.4.1:** item 3's delay moves the whole ladder only for scope `wake_up`; scope
> `alarms` moves only the early rung, to N min after the new last alarm (item 7). A "wake up"
> appointment before the shift ends replaces the shift for that morning (item 11). Your own alarm
> on a day off or leave can have a ladder of type `own` (item 14, status `idle_own_off` when off).
> `done` no longer stands: a finished morning goes straight to the next state (item 19).

### The morning lock and its two exceptions

From the early rung until 60 minutes after the late rung, nothing moves the ladder, except:

1. **Item 3 (change this morning)** moves all three times by the same amount as the wake-up, later
   or earlier. It's the only thing that can move them later.
   - The delay **persists** through any recalculation that morning (HA restart, `current_shift`
     update, re-plan); it doesn't snap back to the calendar times.
   - It's cleared when that morning's ladder ends (late + 60 min).
   - Works any time that morning: while snoozing before the early rung, or after you're up.
2. **Worse weather or traffic** moves the times **earlier only**:
   - HA checks `weather.forecast_home` every 15 minutes from 2 hours before the early rung until
     leave-by. If the condition for the drive gets worse, leave-by moves earlier by that
     condition's minutes from the app's weather settings (sent with each plan), minus what the plan
     already added (no double counting).
   - The phone re-checks the drive when an alarm is stopped (as the leave-by reminder already
     does); a longer drive moves the times earlier the same way.
   - Better weather or traffic never moves them later.
   - Only rungs still ahead move. A rung that would land in the past is published as **now + 60
     s**: the Backstop's time trigger never fires a time already past (HA fact sheet, answer 1).
     One dry run before cutover proves the 60 s lead fires. From v0.5 the integration fires a due
     rung directly instead.
   - Stacks on top of an item 3 delay.

An HA restart reloads the stored times unchanged.

### What turns it off

Only these, otherwise it stays armed (fail loud):
- The shift override set to the literal `Off` (including a shift skipped the night before).
- A leave day (`deadline_source: leave`).
- A morning type not covered.
- The shift deleted from the calendar.

Item 3 with "no alarms" does **not** turn it off while the calendar shows the shift.

### Recalculated on

HA start (after the bridge loads), every `planned`, any state change of `sensor.current_shift` or the
override, `cancelled`, `skipped`, `unskipped`, `bedtime_undone`, and once at late + 60 min to roll
over to the next morning. Never leaves the ladder wiped (recompute after `empty_state()`), and
always respects the lock and its exceptions.

The `is_home()` gate is removed from **publishing** the times, and shift mornings no longer depend on
`safety_net_causes`. Whether a rung fires while you're away is still the blueprint's call.

### What the plan sends for the ladder

The app adds an explicit `ladder` block to `planned`, instead of HA guessing from `alarm_cause`:
`type` (`shift` | `appointment` | `none`), `leave_by`, `first_day_back`, and the per-condition
weather minutes from the app's settings.

### Each rung sensor shows

`leave_by`, `leave_by_source` (`plan` | `calendar`), `first_day_back`, `shift_start` or the
appointment, `delay_minutes`, `changed_by` and `reason` (item 3), `weather_extra_minutes` and the
condition. The Bedtime Readout and the watchdog read these instead of re-deriving them.

Rung sensors read `unavailable` whenever the ladder isn't armed.

### Ladder status

New `sensor.bedtime_bridge_ladder_status`, needed at item 6's cutover (not later): without it the
watchdog and readout treat every deliberate "off" as a fault and send a critical "DISARMED" push and
a spoken warning.

- States: `armed`, `done`, `idle_override_off`, `idle_leave`, `idle_uncovered`, `idle_no_shift`,
  `fault` (v0.5 adds `firing`). v0.4.1 adds `idle_own_off` (item 14) and makes `done` momentary
  (item 19).
- Attributes: `reason`, `morning`, `next_rung`.
- Watchdog v1-30 and Readout v1-30 (HA side) stay quiet on any `idle_*` state, using the same
  two-positive-values pattern they use for leave.

### Interim Backstop changes until v0.5

The Backstop blueprint keeps firing in v0.4, so its instance needs (HA side, as hand-offs):
- **Gate on ladder status `armed`** instead of `current_shift`'s `effective_shift` in `[Days]`, so
  appointment mornings fire.
- **A presence gate** before firing: away means nothing fires (unknown counts as home). Note
  `person.michael_perschy` includes `device_tracker.macbook`, so a MacBook left at home reads
  home; consider dropping it from the person or gating on the iPhone tracker. The same applies to
  the integration's "only when home".

### Cutover and rollback (kept from Phase 2)

Three consumers read `sensor.wake_rung_*` today and move together:
`automation.wake_deadline_backstop` (inputs `rung_early_sensor`, `rung_deadline_sensor`,
`rung_late_sensor`), `automation.shift_calendar_watchdog` (input `rung_sensors`) and
`script.bedtime_readout` (hardcoded in `/config/scripts/`).

Before cutover:
1. Read back the integration's rung and ladder status entity ids with `ha_search`; never assume
   them.
2. "Not armed" is `unavailable` on the rungs plus an `idle_*` ladder status. Watchdog v1-30 and
   Readout v1-30 are in place and quiet on every `idle_*` reason (Readout `dry_run: true` check).
3. The Backstop instance gates on ladder status `armed` and on presence (see "Interim Backstop
   changes").
4. Dry run: a rung moved to now + 60 s fires.
5. Soak: the new rungs and `wake_rung_*` show the same times on at least three Days mornings,
   including one first day back (which differs by design).

Rollback: point the inputs back at `sensor.wake_rung_*` and restore the previous readout file.
`current_shift.yaml` keeps publishing its rungs throughout, so rollback needs no code.

### Out of scope for item 6

Firing the rungs (HA project phase 3), taking over `current_shift.yaml` (phase 4), retiring the
watchdog or Wake Confirm, and Swings.

## 7. Sleep In has one owner: the integration

Today both the Bedtime Readout (`auto_set_sleep_in: true`) and the integration (`_sync_sleep_in` on
every plan) set `input_boolean.sleep_in`. They agree today (both use sunrise + 15 min), but item 3
changes Sleep In in the morning, so only the integration should own it.

- The integration keeps setting it: on every plan, on skip, on undo bedtime, and on item 3.
- HA side: the Readout instance sets `auto_set_sleep_in: false` (a hand-off, read back after).

**Done when:** only the integration changes Sleep In; the Readout reads it but doesn't set it.

## 8. End this morning's alarms from Tonight

Added 27 Oct 2026. For when you're up without having ended the series on the Stop screen: the
first alarm went off and you're up, or two are snoozing and the third hasn't rung, or you woke
before any of them.

- Tonight shows **End this morning's alarms** from 2 hours before the first alarm (a setting in
  the app) until the series is over, listing what's left ("4:25 snoozing until 4:34 · 4:30 still to ring").
- The same slide-and-hold as item 2 (5 s). Same effect as item 2's end-all: snoozing alarms stop,
  ones still to ring are cancelled, no "are you up?" check, one `stopped` with `all: true`,
  temporary alarms and other mornings stay, the backstops don't move.
- **Before the first alarm** it reads "I'm up early" and the `stopped` message carries `early:
  true`. Home Assistant turns on a separate helper (option *Turn on when I end the alarms before
  they ring*, default the same as the stop helper, `input_boolean.wake_mike`) instead of the stop
  helper, so a gentler wake can be set up (HA side: a second Wake Sequence instance with its own
  helper and a longer Wake Transition). Good Morning still runs (`RUN_GOOD_MORNING`).
- Unlike *Skip …'s wake-up* (the night before; turns the shift failsafes off), this means "I'm up":
  the backstops stay.
- Afterwards **Back to bed** (item 3) is available, e.g. to set later alarms after waking early.

**Done when:** from Tonight you can end the rest of this morning's alarms, before or during the
wake-up, without opening the Stop screen; an early end runs the early helper.

## 9. The wake routines run once per wake-up

Added 27 Oct 2026. Wake Sequence treats `input_boolean.wake_mike` as a button (it turns it back off
at once), so every stop re-runs it: lights to full and Sleep In cleared again, and Good Morning is
pushed again.

- The stop helper (or item 8's early helper) and `RUN_GOOD_MORNING` go out only on the **first**
  stop of a wake-up. Later stops that morning update the sensors and fire `stopped` as before.
- The snooze helper isn't turned on once the wake routine has run, or while it's already on (Snooze
  Fade ignores a repeat anyway).
- "Once" resets whenever a new series is set: a plan, item 3, or *Not going to sleep yet*.
- Test alarms are unchanged (they always run the routines).

**Done when:** stopping the second and third alarm after the first doesn't re-run Wake Mike or
re-send Good Morning.

# v0.5: the integration fires the ladder (HA Phase 3)

Adopted from the HA project's "Phase 3 spec: the integration fires the ladder" (27 Oct 2026), which
is the source of truth for the details: scheduler, rung actions (same as Backstop v1-10), deadline
loop as a restart-safe timer, catch-up after restart, soft nudges replacing Wake Confirm, presence
gate, ladder status (adds `firing`), shadow/live mode, cutover and rollback.

Decisions from the app side:
- `appointment_firing` default **`same`** as Days (the loud actions only fire while still in bed).
- Sleep In **no longer suppresses** the soft nudges; only item 6's four switches turn the ladder off.
  Item 3 stops them.
- Soft nudges never start after a **test alarm** stop or a **temporary alarm** stop.
- A rung that is due or past and unfired fires at once (replaces v0.4's now + 60 s).
- v0.4's interim pieces retire: the off/on restart of Wake Confirm and the Backstop, and the
  Backstop's interim gates (the blueprints are turned off after the shadow soak).

What the app provides (Phase 3's list, items 7–9):
- A message for item 3, `morning_changed`: `delay_minutes`, new alarm times (or none), Sleep In,
  `reason`, note, `changed_by`.
- `stopped` with `all: true` (item 2).
- The `ladder` block in `planned` (item 6) tells a shift morning from an appointment.

# v0.6: the integration reads the calendar (HA Phase 4)

Adopted from the HA project's "Phase 4 spec: the integration reads the calendar" (27 Oct 2026):
the integration reads `calendar.calendar` itself as the HA-side fallback (next shift, leave, first
day back), keeps a Store cache with a 3-day stale ceiling, publishes `sensor.bedtime_bridge_shift`
with the same attributes as `sensor.current_shift`, and self-checks the ladder at 18:00 with Repairs
and a critical push. `current_shift.yaml`, `sensor.wake_rung_*` and the Shift Calendar Watchdog then
retire, after a 14-day shadow.

Decisions from the app side:
- Shift titles match after trimming, **case-insensitive**.
- A calendar read failing for more than **6 h** raises a warning Repair.
- Appointments stay the app's job; HA never guesses them from the calendar.
- Item 6's `calendar_entity` option (first-day-back lookback) is the same option Phase 4 uses.
- Nothing new is needed from the app.
