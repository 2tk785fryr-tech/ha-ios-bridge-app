# Bedtime Bridge v0.4.2

Received 29 Oct 2026. App and integration (0.4.2). Item 4 is not decided: not built. Mockups were
reviewed in the cloud chat; the descriptions here are the spec.

## 1. Leave checks survive "change this morning"

- Today: bridge.py cancels all RUN_LEAVE_CHECK pushes on any morning_changed (and clears
  leave_reminders), so a snow delay or a sleep-longer change gets no fresh drive times.
- scope "wake_up" (shift entire wake-up): move Home Assistant's leave_by by delay_minutes and
  reschedule the checks from the new leave-by (same minutes before it).
- scope "alarms" (shift alarms only): leave-by unchanged; keep the checks as they are.
- "No alarms" on a work morning: keep the checks while the calendar still shows the shift; stop
  only if the morning is skipped, cancelled or the shift is deleted.
- Skip, cancel and undo bedtime still stop them.
- The app's own fallback notifications follow the same rule.
- Tests: wake_up +120 → checks rescheduled 120 min later; alarms +30 → unchanged; no alarms with
  the shift on the calendar → kept; skipped → stopped.

## 2. Setting for how far ahead "Wake at a different time" starts

- Today the time picker starts at 6 hours from now (then rounded up to the interval, v0.4.1 item
  18).
- Settings → Alarms: "Start new alarms this far from now": 1–12 hours, 1-hour steps, default 6.
  Rounding still applies after it (8 h from 10:52 PM = 6:52 → 7:00 with 15-min rounding).
- Everywhere that sheet is used. "8 hours from now" in Sleep later stays its own choice.

## 3. Nap

- Settings → Nap: "Nap" on/off (off by default; the button only shows when on); number of alarms
  (default 2) and minutes apart (default 10); get-ready buffer before events WITH an address
  (default 30 min); buffer before events WITHOUT an address (default 0; no get-ready, you may just
  get up and stay home); the quick choices (default 20, 30, 45 min, 1, 1.5, 2 hr, editable) plus
  "Other…".
- Tap a length → alarms set straight away (first alarm = now + length; the rest at the set
  spacing).
- Conflict check before showing the lengths, against everything later today and up to the next 12
  hours (shifts, events marked wake up, and any other event):
  - shifts and events with an address: latest wake = start − arrive-early buffer − drive time now
    (usual service and fallback) − nap get-ready buffer;
  - events with no address: latest wake = start − the no-address buffer.

  The LAST nap alarm must be at or before the latest wake, and not after the next regular alarm.
  Lengths that break it are greyed out ("too late") with the reason in an amber box ("Dentist at
  3:00 PM, 25 min drive. Leave by 2:25, ready 30 min before, so your last nap alarm can be 1:55 at
  the latest"), plus a purple button for the longest nap that fits ("Nap 43 min · 1:45 & 1:55").
  "Other…" enforces the same limit.
- Nap alarms behave like temporary alarms: snooze/stop as usual; no wake routines, Good Morning or
  backstops; Sleep In and Awake For Day untouched; regular and temporary alarms stay.
- Home Assistant: nap_started (alarm times) / nap_ended messages and events; option "Napping
  helper" (e.g. input_boolean.napping) on at start, off at end, only while home (house automations
  on it are Michael's, HA side); phone commands sent home or away: RUN_NAP_START and RUN_NAP_END,
  for new shortcuts "Nap Start - App" (Report Started, nap Focus on, Report Completed) and "Nap End
  - App" (Focus off, Report). Document both and their automations in docs/shortcuts.md.
- Layout:
  - Nap screen: title "How long?", "2 alarms, 10 min apart · tap to set", a two-column grid of
    lengths each showing its alarm times ("20 min · 1:22 & 1:32"), greyed "too late" ones, the
    amber box, the purple "longest that fits" button, "Other…".
  - During a nap: a "Napping" card at the top of the main screen ("Up at 1:32", "2 alarms set ·
    1:32 1:42", "Nap Focus on · napping helper on", End nap / Change…).
  - The Nap button itself lives in the bottom bar (item 6).

## 5. Alarm card on the main screen: clean header, coloured rings per alarm

- Header: "✓ 3 alarms set" in green, no times (they're in the bubbles). Stays "N alarms set"
  through the morning. In Preview (nothing set): "Preview · not set" in grey.
- Each bubble's outline shows its real state (from AlarmKit):
  - set, still to ring: solid green outline (same green as the header);
  - snoozing: yellow outline, pulsing, label under it "snoozing · rings 04:29";
  - turned off: red outline, time dimmed and crossed out, label "off";
  - should be set but isn't: red DASHED outline, label "not set".

  The wake-up alarm keeps its purple fill with the outline on top. Preview: no outline.
- After the last alarm is off, the rings stay until the morning is over (late backstop + 60 min),
  then the card shows the next plan with plain bubbles until those are set.
- Same states for nap and temporary alarms. Reduce Motion: steady yellow, no pulse.

## 6. Bottom bar: same size as today, changes with the moment

- A top row (status, or the one main action) and a bottom row of two buttons plus "⋯". The bar
  never grows.
- States:
  1. Daytime, nothing set for tonight: "Tomorrow 4:30 · not set yet" · Nap · Preview plan
  2. Evening, plan ready: [Set 3 alarms · up 4:30] (purple) · Preview plan · Nap
  3. Alarms set, before bed:
     - within the "Not ready for bed" window (item 7): "✓ 3 alarms set" · Not ready for bed ·
       Sleep later
     - after that: "✓ 3 alarms set" · Sleep later · Edit alarms

     Nap is hidden once the bedtime alarms are set.
  4. Plans changed (calendar, override, missing alarm): [Calendar changed · re-plan] (purple) ·
     Preview plan · Edit alarms. Takes the top row whenever it applies.
  5. Up early (item 7 window): "3 alarms set · first at 4:20" · I'm up early · Sleep later
  6. Snoozing: "Snoozing · rings 4:29" (yellow) · End alarms · Wake later… (opens change this
     morning)
  7. Up, morning not over (item 7 window): "Alarms off · leave by 5:45" · Back to bed · Preview
     plan
  8. Napping: "Napping · up at 1:32" · End nap · Change nap
- "⋯" opens one list in every state, hiding what doesn't apply: Preview plan, Edit alarms, Sleep
  later…, Nap, Change this morning, Skip <day>'s wake-up, Not going to sleep yet, Add a temporary
  alarm, Settings. Nothing needs scrolling.
- Protected actions, also from "⋯": Not ready for bed, I'm up early, End alarms, Sleep later, End
  nap, Change nap, Wake later / Change this morning, Back to bed, Skip, and saving Edit alarms.
  Shown as amber buttons.
  - Tapping one replaces the WHOLE bar (both rows, same total height) with a caption line saying
    what will happen ("End the rest of this morning's alarms"), an ✕ to back out, and today's
    HoldSlider (slide across, hold 5 s, ring fills, ticks each second; letting go springs back
    and does nothing).
  - Knob colour/icon: red stop (End alarms, End nap); amber moon (Not ready for bed, I'm up early,
    Back to bed); purple clock (Sleep later, Wake later, Change nap, Edit alarms save, Skip).
  - Flows that open a screen put the slider at the commit step; Change this morning keeps its
    second hold and Face ID.
- The cards further down keep their own buttons for now.

## 7. Time windows for the situational options, in one Settings page

- Today "Back to bed" stays available for 12 hours after the last alarm (backToBedAvailable: last
  + 12 h); it still showed in the afternoon after an appointment.
- Settings → Alarms → "When options show", one row each (steppers, 1–12 h):
  - I'm up early / End this morning's alarms: from 2 h before the first alarm (the existing
    setting, moved here).
  - Not ready for bed: for 2 h after bedtime ran (its own setting).
  - Back to bed: until 3 h after the wake-up (the series' last alarm) or after the last stop,
    whichever is later.
  - Sleep later: shown as "until the first alarm" (no setting).

  Footer: "These decide when each option shows in the bottom bar, the ⋯ menu and the cards."
- The bottom bar, the "⋯" list and the cards all use these windows. Once a window closes, the
  option disappears; nothing else changes.
