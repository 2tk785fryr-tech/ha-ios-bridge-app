## Items

1. **Critical alerts from the app's own notifications.** Today only the notifications Home
   Assistant sends can be Critical (through the Home Assistant app). The app's own (leave-by
   reminders, Tomorrow changed, Nap won't fit, Appointment has no location) can be at most
   Time-sensitive, because Critical needs the `com.apple.developer.usernotifications.critical-alerts`
   entitlement, which Apple grants by request only.
   - **Michael:** submits the request (developer.apple.com → Contact Us → Critical Alerts
     entitlement request; account already signed in in the Claude browser). Claude drafts the
     text: what the app does (shift wake-ups with house backstops), which notifications would be
     critical and why (missing leave-by on a work morning), that each one's level is the user's
     choice in Settings → Notifications. Claude doesn't submit it.
   - **Once granted:** add the entitlement (and the profile), ask for critical-alert permission
     with notifications, and offer Critical for the app's own notifications in Settings →
     Notifications (defaults unchanged). If permission is off, they fall back to Time-sensitive.
   - **If Apple says no or doesn't answer:** nothing changes; the item stays open.

2. **A backup phone reminder when Home Assistant is down.** With drive checks in Home Assistant,
   the app skips its own leave reminders while you're home, so if Home Assistant is down overnight
   that morning gets none. Keep one phone reminder at leave time as a backup: set at bedtime with
   the alarms, it's withdrawn once Home Assistant's own leave check for that morning has gone out
   (or the morning is cancelled, changed to no alarms or you've left), and it says it wasn't
   re-checked ("Leave by 5:48 · not re-checked: Home Assistant didn't answer").
   - To decide: only the leave-time one, or each reminder time; how the app learns Home
     Assistant's check went out (the check's reply, or the app asks on wake).

3. **API keys in the integration.** (Michael asked whether they can be made secure; proposal
   below, to keep, change or drop.)
   - **Where they are now:** the Apple push key (.p8), the Apple Maps key, the TomTom and Google
     keys sit in the integration's options, which Home Assistant keeps in plain text in
     `.storage/core.config_entries`, like every integration's keys. The fields are masked
     (password fields), but the options form sends the stored keys back to the browser each time
     it opens. Home Assistant has no encrypted store for integrations; encrypting them ourselves
     would need a second key kept on the same disk, which doesn't really protect them.
   - **Found (read-only):** a failed TomTom request's error includes the request address, and
     that has the key in it (`?key=…`); it's written to the log (info, and as a warning when the
     drive check falls back to the phone). Logs can end up in bug reports.
   - **Proposed:** (a) never log a key: strip keys from error text before logging; (b) the options
     form never shows a stored key: a key field is empty with "set · leave empty to keep", plus a
     way to clear it; (c) diagnostics (if added) redact them; (d) `docs/setup.md`: restrict each
     key where it's made (Google: Routes API only; TomTom: only the Routing API; the Apple keys
     are already limited to push and Maps for this team), keep Home Assistant backups encrypted
     (the default since 2025.1), and don't share `.storage`.

4. **Xcode's "2 issues".** Clear them without changing what the app does. Michael's screenshot
   (30 Oct) shows which two:
   - `BedtimeBridgeApp.swift:22`: *Using '_' to ignore the result of a Void-returning function is
     redundant*, in `await MainActor.run { _ = UIApplication.shared.open(url) }` (`open(_:)` returns
     nothing now). Fix: drop the `_ =`.
   - `BedtimeActivities.swift:94`: *No 'async' operations occur within 'await' expression*, in
     `guard let link = await HomeAssistantLinks.current(…)` (`current` isn't async). Fix: drop that
     `await`.

   Also clear, since they'll show once Xcode rebuilds those files: the other 3 warnings from the
   command-line builds:
   - `AppDelegate.swift:17`: the same `_ =` on a Void result.
   - `QuickActions.swift:96`: the quick-action handler passes a non-Sendable
     `UIApplicationShortcutItem` to the main actor (an error under Swift 6).
   - `SettingsDefaults.swift:54`: `Text + Text` is deprecated since iOS 26 (use interpolation).
   - Not an issue now: the project was last upgraded for Xcode 27.1 (`LastUpgradeCheck = 2710`);
     if Xcode offers "Update to recommended settings", look at what it changes before accepting.

5. **Morning weather shows the weather, not just the extra time.** Today the *Morning weather* row
   on Tonight says "No extra time" when the forecast doesn't affect the commute, and "+15 min,
   snow at 6 AM" when it does (`ContentView.weatherText`). Add the plain forecast: the condition
   and the temperature (e.g. "Clear, 38° · no extra time", "Rain, 52° · +15 min").
   - **With a commute** (a shift or an event with a location): the weather at the time of the
     commute (leave-by to arriving), e.g. "Rain, 52° at 5:50".
   - **No event the next morning, but alarms** (a day-off alarm, your own wake-up): the weather at
     wake-up.
   - **No alarms:** the weather in the morning at Settings → Mornings → *Good morning from*
     (8:00 AM by default).
   - From the forecast source already chosen (Home Assistant, Open-Meteo or Apple Weather), in
     °F or °C as the phone is set. If the forecast can't be read, the row says so, as now.

6. **Leave becomes Vacations.** In Settings, the *Leave* page is renamed **Vacations**; inside it
   the list is **Vacation titles** (Michael's stays "Leave", which is what Home Assistant's
   `current_shift.yaml` matches). Instead of one *Add leave*, there's an **Add <title>** for each
   title (e.g. "Add Leave", "Add Vacation", "Add Sick day"), each saving an all-day event with that
   title. The ⋯ list, the quick action and Siri follow (one Add per title in ⋯; Siri asks which
   title when there's more than one).
   - What vacation days do today stays the same: no shift alarms, the backstop ladder off
     (`idle_leave`), your own alarm's backstops off by default.
   - **Also (decided 30 Oct, a–f):**
     a. **Back-to-work reminder** the evening before the first workday after a vacation: "Back to
        work tomorrow: Days at 6:30, first alarm 4:20 (+15 min, first day back)", so the first
        alarm isn't a surprise. (The first-day-back extra time already exists.)
     b. **Mornings on vacation:** Sleep In on for each vacation morning by itself, and the no-alarm
        morning flow (disarm when you leave the bedroom, Up for the day?) as usual.
     c. **Wind-down on vacation:** only before mornings that have an alarm (your own alarm, an
        event); none before mornings without one.
     d. **Travelling:** a switch when adding a vacation (and on the event): "I'm travelling".
        Drives start from where you are, the weather is where you are, and the house is left alone
        (Home Assistant already skips the house when you're away).
     e. **See and change vacations:** the Vacations page lists the ones coming up, with Edit (move
        or shorten, e.g. "back early") and Delete.
     (Per-title behaviour, e.g. sick days, isn't needed: for a sick day Michael deletes that day's
     shift from the calendar.)
     f. **Weekly summary:** weeks with vacation days are marked ("Vacation Nov 3 – 7"), and the
        weekly notification is skipped while you're on vacation (the summary is still there).

7. **Alarms page: the two alarm series as their own pages.** Today Settings → Alarms has the usual
   series (number of alarms, time apart, wake-up time is the last/first) mixed in with snooze,
   sound and the rest, and *Your own alarms* is a separate page further down with its own set
   (same numbers by default, but the wake-up time is the **first** alarm). New order on the Alarms
   page:
   1. **Where alarms come from** (Bedtime Bridge alarms / Clock app), as now, at the top.
   2. **Appointment & event alarms** (a page): number of alarms, time apart, wake-up time is the
      first / last alarm (the settings the usual series has today, same defaults: 3, 10 min,
      last).
   3. **Your own alarms** (a page), right below: as now (3, 10 min, first, plus the rest of that
      page).
   4. Then snooze, sound, when I stop an alarm, when options show, change this morning, look
      ahead, plug in your phone.
   - The Alarms row on the main Settings page shows something that still fits (e.g. "Bedtime
     Bridge alarms · snooze 9 min") instead of "3 alarms, 10 min apart".
   - Shifts set to "use my usual series" now say "same as appointment & event alarms" (they use
     the same settings as today).
   - **The Shortcuts action *Set Wake-Up Alarms*** (a time you give it, for a day off) moves to
     *Your own alarms* (decided 30 Oct): it starts at your time, like *Wake at a different time*.
     Today it uses the usual series and ends at your time. Update its description in Shortcuts,
     the README and `docs/shortcuts.md`.
